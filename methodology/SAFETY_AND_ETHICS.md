# Safety and Ethics

ReactorBench-Eval is intentionally limited to public, safety-oriented engineering questions.

The public dataset excludes:
- proprietary employer/client documents;
- plant-specific confidential calculations or P&IDs;
- physical-protection arrangements or facility vulnerabilities;
- classified information;
- detailed weapons-enabling content.

Reference contexts are based on public standards, public reactor information and original
engineering abstractions. The benchmark evaluates reasoning quality, evidence discipline,
uncertainty and systems thinking rather than access to sensitive information.
